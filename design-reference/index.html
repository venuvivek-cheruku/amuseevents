04 — the process

From "is this a good idea?"
to "that was perfect".

Five quiet steps. No surprises along the way.

01
Listen — week 1

A 20-minute call.

Coffee in Isleworth or video, you pick. We listen, we don't pitch. By the end, we both know if we're a fit.

02
Shape — week 2

One page. One price.

You get a clear plan with a fixed quote. Move things, swap things, change anything. Re-quote in 24 hours.

03
Source — months out

Vendors, venues, the lot.

We bring the people we trust. You meet the ones that matter. Everyone else stays invisible — by design.

04
Rehearse — week of

Walk-through & run-of-day.

Every minute mapped. Every contingency named. Every supplier briefed. You stop checking your phone.

05
Run — day 0

You arrive as a guest.

Your only job is to be present. We hold the room behind you, in earpieces, so quietly you forget we're there.
