— FAQ —

The questions
we hear most.

Do you travel outside London? +

Up to 90 minutes from central London is included in any package. Further afield is on request — we do about 8 destination weddings a year.

Can we mix & match the packages? +

Yes. Every package is a starting shape, not a contract. After the first call we tend to add or drop items so it fits your specific day.

What if our date isn't free? +

We'll be honest within the hour. If we can't take it, we'll recommend three planners we'd happily hand the day to.

How do we hold a date? +

20% deposit after the consultation. Fully refundable for 14 days, no questions asked.

Do you do small weddings? +

Yes — our smallest last year was 14 guests in a Soho speakeasy. Small days deserve as much care as the big ones.
