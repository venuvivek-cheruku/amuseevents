Before you
get in touch.

How quickly do you reply? +

Within 4 hours, including weekends. Usually inside the hour during studio hours.

Do you charge for the consultation? +

No. The first 20 minutes are always free, and there's no deposit until you've seen the quote.

What's your minimum booking? +

Around £450 for a kids party, £1,400 for day-of wedding coordination, £3,200 for corporate. Below that we'll happily refer you to a planner we trust.

Can I see real budgets? +

Yes — once we've had a quick chat, we'll send three real anonymised budgets near your size and shape.

Do you do destination events? +

Within 90 minutes of central London, included. Further (UK or EU), happy to talk.
